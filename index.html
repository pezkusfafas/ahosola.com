Fully Autonomous Content Engine

I engineered a custom codebase that automatically generates and uploads AI-powered acapella and instrumental versions of popular music. The bot autonomously handles song selection and transforms them into high-quality visual and sonic content. During a short trial run, the system generated over one million views in just a few days, validating high market demand for automated digital assets.

Aaria: A Futuristic Brand Concept

Developed the brand identity and industrial concept for "Aaria," a houseware design brand centered on geometric, futuristic aesthetics. The utensil designs utilize a consistent triangular design language—from the striking handle forms to the functional components—creating a cohesive and modular ecosystem. The brand logo was specifically engineered without "holes" to ensure high-fidelity printing and stencil application on physical products.

AI-Driven Video Production & Marketing

I developed proprietary AI software that streamlines professional-grade video post-production at scale. The system utilizes AI transcription to automatically cut silence and artifacts, apply dynamic zooming for engagement, and add background music. It further extracts "viral clips" from the main content and uses the Google API for automated distribution. Contact me for a demo of the software or to see live channels utilizing this pipeline.
